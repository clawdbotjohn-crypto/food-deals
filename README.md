# Food Deals

> Website listing weekly food deals in your area — recurring restaurant specials (Taco Tuesday, half-price sushi Wednesday) organized by day of the week, plus integration with Too Good To Go surplus food deals for discounted/expiring items. Help users find the cheapest meals each day and claim TGTG deals early by detecting schedules and price drops.
